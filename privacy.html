Receipts Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026.

Receipts (listed in TestFlight as AI Fantasy Bot) is a private fantasy football companion for members of one ESPN league. This page explains what the app handles.

What we use

  • League data. Team names, lineups, scores and standings for the league come from ESPN and are shown in the app.
  • ESPN sign-in (optional). If you sign in with ESPN, your ESPN session cookies are stored only on your device, in its secure storage, and sent once to our chat service to confirm which team is yours. They are not saved on our servers. Your ESPN password is entered on ESPN's own page and never reaches the app.
  • Owner chat. If you join the league chat, your messages, reactions and any images you post are stored with our database provider (Supabase) so other league members can see them. You join with an anonymous account tied to your team.
  • Feedback. If you tap thumbs up or down on an answer, that rating and any note you add are stored so we can improve answers.
  • Settings. Theme and similar preferences stay on your device.

What we don't do

No ads, no selling or sharing of data with advertisers, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Settings > Clear all chats removes your assistant conversations from your device. To delete your owner-chat messages or account, email the address below.
